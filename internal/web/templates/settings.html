{{/* The preference forms opt out of hx-boost deliberately. data-theme and data-hover live on , and a boosted form swaps only the body, so the cookie changed but the page kept its old look until the next real navigation. That reads as a button that does nothing. A full navigation costs one round trip and is correct. Every control is a row: its name and one line saying what it does on the left, the control on the right in a column every row shares, so the selects and switches line up. The longer explanation a row used to print in full is behind the (i) beside its name: it is still on the screen for whoever wants it, and no longer between the reader and the control. Each form returns to its own section with a toast naming what was saved (savedNext), so a full-page reload does not drop the reader at the top of the screen wondering whether anything happened. */}} {{define "content"}}
